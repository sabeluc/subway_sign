route descriptions, and special messages. Includes unusual and alternate
services; this is not a current route map or a complete codebook.

The lines below are adapted from an enthusiast transcription by fwilson01:
https://www.reddit.com/r/nycrail/comments/hwndrx/here_is_a_peek_at_the_r44r46_codebookobviously/

Each route line starts with its letter. Two spaces separate text readings.
Spacing after "to" is normalized; other spelling and case are retained.
Not independently photo-verified.
